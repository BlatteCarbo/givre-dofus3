# Combat movement continuation exhaustion — 2026-11-06

A live Sylvanbot defeat prompted analysis of four turns without spells. The combat profile allowed approach and automatic spells and had no challenge constraints, but this alone did not explain the idle turns; no account was relaunched to repeat the lost fight. Trace comparison isolated a specific executor defect: after a plan consisting only of movement was fully consumed, the executor could reuse its now-empty continuation and end the turn without planning from the confirmed position. This may explain a missed attack opportunity on turn four; it does not prove that all four turns would have become offensive or that the fight would have been won.

A regression in `CombatMovementContinuationDispatchTests` reproduced one planner invocation rather than two after confirmed movement. The minimal guard against reusing an exhausted continuation was added. At the snapshot boundary, movement/resource/utility tests were still running against the fix; a separate movement-then-cast control had failed before the change and remained under diagnosis. No post-fix test result, build, deployment, or live validation was yet available. Do not resume Sylvanbot or claim the issue fixed until the run result is reviewed.
