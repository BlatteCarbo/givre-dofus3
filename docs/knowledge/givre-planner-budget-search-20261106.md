# Combat planner search order under a fixed budget — 2026-11-06

A live Sylvanbot defeat triggered investigation of turns where the planner found no attack. Reusing the root cast candidates did not help and was removed. An experiment instead visits candidate firing origins near enemies before origins near the starting position, without changing the search budget or scoring.

The revised ordering found an attack in replay at a 60,000-unit budget: four movement steps followed by spell 12755, with estimated damage 488.3. A minimal regression test established causality: at a 300-unit budget the old ordering returned no actions, while the new ordering found an attack. The test was initially confounded by defensive scoring; setting threat to zero isolated search-budget behavior. Neighboring combat scenarios passed (199 tests in one run; later combined validation was still underway at the snapshot boundary).

This is evidence for improved bounded search on the tested fixture/replay, not proof of a general combat fix. Final validation superseded that provisional status: the old candidate ordering failed the 300-unit-budget regression while the revised ordering passed; all 207 targeted tests passed. The full suite finished with 4,785 passed, one timeout in `MZP0PacketBusTests.Competing_publish_cancel_and_timeout_settle_once_under_load`, and two skipped; the timeout was reported as a pre-existing independent failure, so the full suite was not green. The corrected build was then deployed and Sylvanbot won a live protector fight in seven turns, casting every turn (17 spells injected), with seven confirmed movements and no skipped-cast turn. The fight was in Fast, not Turbo; a challenge failed and enemy-HP tracking briefly differed from server death confirmation. This validates the bounded-search correction for that replay and live fight, not general combat reliability or Turbo.
