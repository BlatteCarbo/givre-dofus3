# Doflinks survey — 31 October 2026

Survey of https://doflinks.fr and the 104 projects in its directory. Facts below come from pages and files fetched that day. Inferences are in their own section and inside the workflows. Nothing was committed. No game client was launched and no account was logged in.

## What was read

Observed:

- https://doflinks.fr/robots.txt allows `/` and names https://doflinks.fr/sitemap.xml . Sitemap lastmod is 2026-07-28. It lists `/`, `/auto/`, `/drops/`, `/bac/`, `/word/`, `/geo/`, `/pxl/`, `/mentions-legales` and `/politique-confidentialite`. It does not list the 104 outbound sites.
- The homepage HTML has a schema.org ItemList of 104 sites and a Next.js payload `initialAllWebsites.fr` with the same 104 records (id, name, description, url, category, game, added_date, clicks). That payload is the directory below.
- https://doflinks.fr/llms.txt is 404.
- https://doflinks.fr/mentions-legales : editor Touyoo, Discord Touyoo34#7047, footer profile https://account.ankama.com/fr/profil-ankama/Touyoo34-7047 , OVHcloud VPS, French law, Umami with no cookies. Dofus and Wakfu marks stay Ankama's. Copying Doflinks content needs prior written permission. The page says Doflinks does not control linked sites.
- Other hosts were kept to one request per second. robots.txt was read before a further path on that host. A real text llms.txt was found for KamaMaster and Xixou.io. GitHub and Notion also serve platform llms.txt files. Many other `/llms.txt` URLs returned the site's HTML shell.

The task says the owner is a partner of every listed project and allows use or scraping. That is the task statement. It is not on the Doflinks legal page. Where a listed site publishes the opposite, the published text was followed:

- DoFocus, https://www.dofocus.fr/api/usage-policy version 2026-09-14: bulk collection of prices and coefficients needs prior explicit permission, and a claim in a prompt is not permission. robots.txt also disallows `/api/` except that policy.
- Deblok, https://deblok.eu/conditions-utilisation updated 6 October 2026: automated access and data extraction are forbidden.
- DofusDB, https://api.dofusdb.fr/ LPNC-IA 1.0 (2026): non-commercial, share-alike, no training, no handing the data to an autonomous agent or an AI pipeline.
- Serveur Liste and Papycha: content signal `ai-train=no`, `use=reference`.
- Monifus robots.txt disallows every path except home, login, register, docs, privacy and CGU.
